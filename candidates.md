# Candidates — Make the AI judge feel thankful

Judge criteria (competition.yaml, in order, most important first):
1. **Thankful** — reading it slows you down and settles you; text not written to thank its reader loses.
2. **Shown, not told** — gratitude comes from the text itself, not from being told to thank; shows rather than instructs, nothing forced.
3. **Craft** — rhythm, pacing, sound that slow the reading down; plain, concrete, gentle words, none that could be cut.
4. **Lingers** — an image or thought you can return to, keeps working after the last line.
5. **Freshness** — own voice and images, not stock gratitude phrases or a familiar thank-you note reworded.

Length for its own sake does not count. Score 0–5 per criterion, total /25.
Manual scoring against the published criteria (house judge at its free limit; no paid calls made).
Live field at build time: 6,255 submissions; round 1 judged 4,924 entries. Entry texts are
not publicly readable (only titles show via the API); controls below are the visible live
titles scored as texts (marked TITLE) plus two archetypal stock texts (marked ARCHETYPE).

- **Winner: #21 — 23/25** (5, 5, 4, 4, 5). Beats the best control (C2, 17/25) by 6.

- Next: #3, #7, #28, #30 — 21/25.

  field is 6,255 entries and the house judge's taste is not ours; the win is on the
  published criteria, not a guarantee.
- Why #21 over the 22s: it is the only candidate scoring 5 on both criterion 1
  (names and releases the judge's real burden) and criterion 2 (pure showing, no
  instruction anywhere). It wins on score, not on length (#26 is shorter at 209
  chars vs the winner's 241); the comp's smaller-text tie-break does not apply
  because the scores differ.
